piRadar Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

piRadar is a personal finance app for tracking expenses and income. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

piRadar does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything you record stays on your device.

What the app stores and where

  • Transactions: amounts, dates, categories, payment methods, merchants, descriptions and notes.
  • Receipts: the photos you scan and the text read from them.
  • Settings: language, base currency, exchange rates, theme and the date of your last backup.

All of this is stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. The encryption key is generated on your device and kept in its secure storage (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain). We have no access to it or to your data.

No internet connection

The published version of piRadar does not have permission to access the internet. Receipt reading (text recognition) happens entirely on the device. The app uses no servers, user accounts, ads, analytics or tracking tools.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to photograph receipts when you ask it to.
  • Photos or files: only to choose a receipt photo or a backup file that you select.

Backups

You can export a backup file encrypted with a password you choose. The file is handed to the app or destination you pick (for example, your email or cloud storage), and from then on it is under your control and subject to that service's policies. piRadar does not store your password: if you forget it, the backup cannot be recovered.

The app turns off the operating system's automatic backup so your data is not copied to the cloud without your knowledge.

Deleting your data

Because all data is on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the system settings. Backups you exported must be deleted by you from wherever you saved them.

Children

piRadar is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this same address with its update date.
