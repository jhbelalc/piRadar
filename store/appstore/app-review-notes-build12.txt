1. SCREEN RECORDING
A screen recording on a physical iPhone was sent in the Resolution Center reply for this app (launch, add a transaction, scan a receipt, analytics, categories, encrypted backup, settings). This build has the same flow and adds an English interface and an opening-balance line. The app has no account registration, login, user-generated content, social features, or paid content/features, so none of those flows exist.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
piRadar is a personal finance app for adults (18+) who want to track their own expenses and income. It solves the problem of seeing where one's money goes without sharing financial data with anyone: the user records transactions (manually or by photographing a receipt), organizes them by category and payment method, and sees reports by day, week, month and year in US dollars (USD) and Colombian pesos (COP). All data stays on the device.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No account, login or credentials are needed: every feature is available as soon as the app opens. The interface follows the device language (English or Spanish; Settings > Language can change it). Main screens: Home, Transactions, Analytics, More (Categories, Payment methods, Exchange rates, Backup, Settings, About).
- Add a transaction: tap "Add" on Home or the green "+", enter the amount, choose a category, tap "Save".
- Scan a receipt: tap "Scan" on Home, then "Take photo" or "Choose from gallery", pick any photo of a printed receipt; the app proposes the total, date and merchant (and "Other expenses" as category if it cannot detect one); the user confirms and saves.
- Each month starts with an "Opening balance" line: what was left from the previous months.
- More > Backup > "Create backup" creates an encrypted backup file with a password the user chooses; "Restore a backup" restores one.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS OR PLATFORMS
None. The app has no server, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no network requests. Everything runs on the device:
- Data is stored in an encrypted SQLite database (SQLite3 Multiple Ciphers, SQLCipher-compatible); the key is generated on the device and kept in the iOS Keychain.
- Receipt text recognition uses Apple's Vision framework on the device (no cloud OCR). The photo and the recognized text are not sent anywhere.
- Backups are files encrypted on the device (AES-256-GCM); the user chooses where to save or share them with the system share sheet.
- UI components: .NET MAUI Blazor Hybrid and MudBlazor (open source, run locally).

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in all regions. It supports two currencies (USD and COP) in every region, with totals kept separate per currency; exchange rates are entered manually by the user.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not applicable. piRadar is a personal record-keeping tool: it does not connect to banks, move money, give loans, trade securities or crypto, or provide financial advice. It contains no protected third-party content (open-source libraries are listed in the app under More > About > Third-party licenses).
