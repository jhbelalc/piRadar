Quick start

How do I start using piRadar?

  1. Tap Add on Home (or the green + button) and enter your first expense or income.
  2. Or tap Scan and take a photo of a receipt: piRadar suggests the total, date and merchant, and you confirm.
  3. See how your month is going on Home and Analytics.
  4. In More > Settings choose the language, the theme and your base currency.
  5. In More > Backup create your first encrypted backup and keep it somewhere safe.

You do not need an account or internet: everything is stored encrypted on your phone.

Adding transactions

How do I add an expense or an income?

  1. Tap Add on Home, or the green + button in the bottom bar.
  2. Choose Expense or Income.
  3. Enter the Amount and choose the currency (COP or USD).
  4. Choose the Category and, if you like, the Payment method, the Date and the Time.
  5. Optional: Merchant, Description and Notes to find the transaction later.
  6. Tap Save.

How do I edit or delete a transaction?

  1. Open Transactions (or the recent transactions on Home) and tap the transaction.
  2. Change what you need and tap Save.
  3. To remove it, tap Delete and confirm. If it had a receipt photo, the photo is deleted too.

Deleting cannot be undone. If in doubt, make a backup first.

How do I filter my transactions?

  1. In Transactions, use the arrows of the period selector to go to the day, week, month or year you want.
  2. Use the All, Expenses and Income filters to see only one type.
  3. To check whether you already recorded an expense, tap the magnifier in Transactions and type a word from the merchant, description or notes, or an amount. It searches your whole history, ignoring capitals and accents.
  4. Choose a Category to see only that one (subcategories included); you can combine it with text. Clear the search or tap the magnifier again to go back to the period.

Scanning receipts

How do I scan a receipt?

  1. Tap Scan on Home.
  2. Tap Take photo or Choose from gallery.
  3. Wait a few seconds: the receipt is read on the phone, without internet.
  4. Check the total, date, merchant and category that piRadar suggests.
  5. Fix anything that is off and tap Save: the transaction and the receipt photo are saved.

piRadar only suggests: nothing is saved until you confirm.

The total is wrong, what do I do?

  1. Look at the Another total? chips under the amount: tap the right one.
  2. If none is right, type the amount by hand.
  3. Tap View text read to see exactly what the phone read.
  4. The warnings about the total, the date or the category tell you what to check before saving.

How do I take a photo that reads well?

  1. Put the whole receipt in the photo, straight and on a flat surface.
  2. Use good light and avoid shadows and glare.
  3. If the paper is wrinkled, smooth it out first.

The camera or gallery does not open

  1. Open the phone settings, find piRadar and turn on the camera and photos permission.
  2. Meanwhile you can use Enter manually.

Categories and payment methods

How do I create a category or a subcategory?

  1. Go to More > Categories and choose Expenses or Income.
  2. Tap +, enter the Name and choose the icon and color.
  3. For a subcategory, choose the main category in Inside.
  4. Tap Save.

What happens if I delete a category or payment method?

  1. If it has no transactions, it is deleted.
  2. If it has transactions or subcategories, it is archived: it no longer appears when adding new transactions, but old ones keep it.
  3. Turn on Show archived to see it and Restore to use it again.

How do I add a payment method?

  1. Go to More > Payment methods and tap +.
  2. Enter the Name, choose the Type and the icon.
  3. To tell two cards apart, enter the Last 4 digits: the full number is never stored.

Analytics and balance

How do I see where my money goes?

  1. Open Analytics.
  2. Choose the period: Day, Week, Month or Year, and move with the arrows.
  3. Choose the currency and switch between Expenses and Income.
  4. See the bars over time, By category, By payment method and the average spending per day.

What is the opening balance?

  1. It is what was left from all previous periods (income minus expenses), per currency.
  2. That is why a new month does not start at zero: it starts with the previous balance.
  3. It is calculated automatically: if you fix or delete an old transaction, it updates. It does not count as income or expense.

How do I see everything in one currency?

  1. In Analytics, if you have transactions in two currencies, choose All in and your base currency.
  2. To convert, piRadar uses the rates you entered in More > Exchange rates.
  3. Transactions with no rate for their date are left out of the total with a warning; tap Add rate to fill it in.

How do I enter an exchange rate?

  1. Go to More > Exchange rates and tap +.
  2. Choose the From and To currencies, enter the rate and the date in Applies from.
  3. Tap Save. Each transaction uses the latest rate dated on or before it.

Rates are only used in the combined view; the totals per currency do not change.

Backup

How do I make a backup?

  1. Go to More > Backup and tap Create backup.
  2. Choose a password and confirm it.
  3. Save the .piradar file wherever you like (email, cloud, files) using the share menu.

The password is not stored anywhere: if you forget it, the backup cannot be opened. Write it down somewhere safe.

How do I restore a backup or move my data to another phone?

  1. Install piRadar on the new phone and move the .piradar file to it.
  2. Go to More > Backup and tap Restore a backup.
  3. Choose the file and enter the password you used to create it.
  4. Review the summary and confirm with Replace. This replaces all the data on that phone.

Why do I see a backup reminder?

  1. Home reminds you when you have never made a backup or more than 30 days have passed since the last one.
  2. Tap Back up now to create it right away.

Settings

How do I change the language, theme or base currency?

  1. Go to More > Settings.
  2. In Language choose Spanish, English or Same as system.
  3. In Theme choose Light, Dark or the system one.
  4. In Base currency choose the currency in which you want to see combined totals.
  5. In Home title you can change the title of the Home screen, and Reset puts the original back.

Privacy and data

Where is my data stored?

  1. Only on your phone, in an encrypted database. There is no account, cloud, ads or tracking.
  2. The app does not use the internet: receipt text is read on the phone itself.
  3. That is why we cannot recover your data if you lose your phone: make backups.

Troubleshooting

I forgot my backup password

  1. It cannot be recovered: the backup is encrypted with that password and piRadar does not keep it.
  2. Try the passwords you usually use, with and without accents and capital letters.
  3. If you still have the app with your data, create a new backup with a password you write down.

The app says it cannot read my data when I open it

  1. This happens when the encrypted data can no longer be opened with the phone's key, for example after restoring the phone from a copy.
  2. If you have a .piradar backup, tap Start over… on that screen and then use Restore a backup.
  3. Erase and start over removes what is in the app: the screen asks you to confirm first.
